# Requirements Document

## 1. Application Overview

### 1.1 Application Name
Heat Rush 2.0

### 1.2 Application Description
Heat Rush 2.0 is a mobile arcade game combining match-3 mechanics with temperature-based strategy and spatial puzzle elements. Players control a 12×24 grid in portrait mode divided into two zones: a preparation zone (rows 1-8) where players can change cube colors and move cubes both horizontally and vertically to form matching patterns without triggering matches, and an automatic zone (rows 9-24) where cubes move upward automatically and matches trigger when aligned. Cubes spawn at the bottom and move upward. Players tap to cycle colors and swipe in any direction to reposition cubes within the preparation zone. Once a complete figure (3+ connected cubes of same color) crosses the separator line into row 9, it accelerates to 2× speed and matches can trigger. Rapid tapping (8 consecutive taps) heats a cube to red, making it a universal wildcard. Flying cubes move horizontally across the upper zone, creating dynamic matching opportunities. The game features enhanced visual effects, gradient materials, grid overlay, reduced screen shake, and progressive difficulty systems.

## 2. Users and Usage Scenarios

### 3.4 Game Screen
**Purpose**: Core gameplay experience

**Layout**:
- 12×24 grid occupying central screen area with visible thin grid lines on dark background
- Grid divided into two zones:
  - Preparation Zone: Rows 1-8 (y=1 to y=8)
  - Automatic Zone: Rows 9-24 (y=9 to y=24)
- Horizontal separator line between row 8 and row 9 (bright yellow, semi-transparent, 2-pixel thickness)
- Top HUD: Current wave, score, combo counter
- Bottom area: Cube spawn zone (y=0, x=1-12)
- Preparation zone: Player can change colors and move cubes horizontally and vertically (y=1-8)
- Automatic zone: Cubes move upward automatically and matches trigger (y=9-24)
- Upper area: Flying cube zone (y=19-23)
- Top boundary: y=24 (cubes stop and accumulate here)
- Pause button in top corner

**Visual Design**:
- Grid lines: Thin, semi-transparent white lines (opacity 0.15) forming cell boundaries
- Zone separator: Bright yellow horizontal line at y=8/y=9 boundary (opacity 0.6, 2-pixel thickness)
- Background: Dark gradient (deep blue to black)
- Cube materials: Gradient-based with smooth color transitions, glossy finish
- Color palette:
  - Yellow: Gradient from bright yellow (#FFD700) to golden orange (#FFA500)
  - Blue: Gradient from cyan (#00CED1) to deep blue (#1E90FF)
  - Green: Gradient from lime (#32CD32) to emerald (#50C878)
  - White: Gradient from pure white (#FFFFFF) to light gray (#E0E0E0)
  - Red: Gradient from bright red (#FF4500) to deep crimson (#DC143C)

**Core Mechanics**:

**Cube System**:
- Cubes spawn randomly at y=0 across x-positions 1-12
- Each cube spawns in a specific x-position and moves upward in that vertical lane
- Spawn interval: 500ms at game start, decreasing to 200ms by wave 10
- Move upward one cell every 1200ms (base speed)
- Four base colors: Yellow, Blue, Green, White
- Color distribution: 25% each color on spawn
- Maximum 288 cubes can exist on grid (12 columns × 24 rows)
- Cube appearance: Gradient material with subtle glow effect, rounded edges

**Zone System**:
- Preparation Zone (y=1 to y=8):
  - Player can tap cubes to change colors
  - Player can swipe cubes horizontally to move them left or right
  - Player can swipe cubes vertically to move them up or down
  - Cubes continue moving upward automatically while in this zone
  - Matches do NOT trigger in this zone (no cube removal)
  - This is a figure preparation area for strategic positioning
- Automatic Zone (y=9 to y=24):
  - Cubes move upward automatically
  - No player interaction allowed (taps and swipes ignored)
  - Matches trigger when 3+ same-colored cubes align
  - Matched cubes explode and are removed
- Zone transition:
  - When a complete figure (3+ connected same-colored cubes) fully crosses from y=8 to y=9, it accelerates to 2× speed
  - A figure is considered complete when all cubes are edge-connected (not diagonal) and surrounded by empty cells or diagonally adjacent cubes
  - Acceleration only occurs when the last cube of the figure crosses the separator line
  - Visual feedback: Brief flash effect when figure crosses separator line
  - Individual cubes or incomplete figures do not accelerate

**Color Cycling Mechanic (Preparation Zone Only)**:
- Single tap on cube in y=1-8: Cycle to next color in sequence (Yellow → Blue → Green → White → Yellow)
- Color change is instant with smooth gradient transition animation
- No limit on color changes per cube
- Visual feedback: Brief glow pulse effect on color change
- Taps on cubes in y=9-24 are ignored

**Horizontal Movement Mechanic (Preparation Zone Only)**:
- Swipe left/right gesture on cube in y=1-8: Move cube horizontally
- Swipe left: Move cube one cell to the left (x-1)
- Swipe right: Move cube one cell to the right (x+1)
- Movement is instant with smooth slide animation
- Movement blocked if:
  - Target cell is occupied by another cube
  - Target position is outside grid (x<1 or x>12)
- Cube continues moving upward after horizontal movement
- No limit on horizontal movements per cube while in preparation zone
- Visual feedback: Slide animation with motion trail
- Swipes on cubes in y=9-24 are ignored

**Vertical Movement Mechanic (Preparation Zone Only)**:
- Swipe up/down gesture on cube in y=1-8: Move cube vertically
- Swipe up: Move cube one cell upward (y+1)
- Swipe down: Move cube one cell downward (y-1)
- Movement is instant with smooth slide animation
- Movement blocked if:
  - Target cell is occupied by another cube
  - Target position is outside preparation zone (y<1 or y>8)
- Cube continues automatic upward movement after vertical repositioning
- No limit on vertical movements per cube while in preparation zone
- Visual feedback: Slide animation with motion trail
- Swipes on cubes in y=9-24 are ignored

**Heating Mechanic (Preparation Zone Only)**:
- 8 consecutive taps on same cube within 2 seconds: Cube turns Red (heated state)
- Red cube acts as universal wildcard for matching
- Tap counter resets if:
  - More than 2 seconds pass between taps
  - Player taps different cube
  - Cube participates in a match
  - Cube crosses into automatic zone (y=9)
- Visual feedback: Tap counter appears above cube (1/8, 2/8, etc.)
- Red cube glows with pulsing red-orange particles and heat wave distortion effect
- Heating only possible in preparation zone (y=1-8)

**Figure Definition and Completion**:
- A figure consists of 3 or more same-colored cubes connected by edges (not diagonals)
- Edge connection: Two cubes share a common side (horizontally or vertically adjacent)
- Diagonal connection does NOT count as part of same figure
- A figure is considered complete when:
  - All cubes in the figure are edge-connected to at least one other cube in the figure
  - No additional same-colored cubes are edge-connected to the figure
  - Surrounding cells are either empty or contain cubes of different colors (diagonal adjacency allowed)
- Examples:
  - 3 yellow cubes in horizontal line = complete figure
  - 4 blue cubes in L-shape (edge-connected) = complete figure
  - 2 green cubes edge-connected + 1 green cube diagonally adjacent = 2 separate entities (incomplete)

**Figure Acceleration System**:
- Acceleration trigger condition: Complete figure fully crosses separator line from y=8 to y=9
- Full crossing definition: Every cube in the figure must have y≥9
- Acceleration effect: All cubes in the figure move at 2× speed (600ms per cell instead of 1200ms)
- Partial crossing: If only some cubes of a figure cross the line, acceleration does NOT occur
- Last cube rule: Acceleration activates only when the last cube of the figure crosses into y=9
- Motivation: Encourages players to create smaller, compact figures for faster progression
- Visual feedback: Speed lines or motion blur effect on accelerated figures
- Accelerated figures maintain 2× speed until they participate in a match or reach y=24

**Match System (Automatic Zone Only)**:
- Match condition: 3 or more cubes of same color aligned horizontally, vertically, or in Tetris-like shapes
- Horizontal match: 3+ cubes in same row (same y-coordinate)
- Vertical match: 3+ cubes in same column (same x-coordinate)
- Shape match: Cubes forming recognizable Tetris shapes (L-shape, T-shape, Z-shape, Square)
- Match detection occurs every frame for all cubes in automatic zone (y≥9) only
- Matches do NOT trigger in preparation zone (y=1-8)
- When match detected in automatic zone:
  - Matched cubes display flashing outline effect for 0.5 seconds
  - Outline color matches cube color with increased brightness
  - Flash frequency: 3 pulses per 0.5 seconds
  - After outline animation, cubes explode with particle burst
  - Points awarded based on match size and shape
  - Combo counter increments
  - Reduced screen shake effect (33% of original intensity)
  - Cubes above the cleared row fall down to fill the gap
- Red cube matching rule: Counts as any color needed to complete match
- Multiple matches can occur simultaneously (cascade scoring)
- Matches can form with any combination of moving, stopped, and flying cubes in automatic zone

**Match Visual Effects**:
- 3-cube match: Single outline flash, small particle burst
- 4-cube match: Double outline flash, medium particle burst with sparkles
- 5+ cube match: Triple outline flash, large particle burst with explosion wave
- Shape match: Special outline pattern following shape contour, enhanced particle effects
- Outline thickness: 3 pixels, semi-transparent glow
- Particle colors match cube colors with trailing effects
- Screen shake reduced to 33% of original intensity

**Gravity After Match**:
- When horizontal match clears cubes in a row, cubes above fall down one cell
- When vertical match clears cubes in a column, cubes above fall down to fill gap
- When shape match clears cubes, all cubes above cleared positions fall down
- Falling cubes move at 2× normal speed (600ms per cell)
- Falling can create new matches (cascade effect)
- Cascade matches award full points and maintain combo

**Scoring System**:
- 3-cube match: 30 points
- 4-cube match: 60 points
- 5-cube match: 100 points
- 6-cube match: 150 points
- 7+ cube match: 200 points + 50 per additional cube
- Shape match bonus: +50 points for L-shape, T-shape, Z-shape; +100 points for Square
- Combo multiplier: ×1.5 for 2nd consecutive match, ×2.0 for 3rd, ×2.5 for 4th+
- Combo resets after 3 seconds without new match
- Red cube in match: +20 bonus points
- Cascade match: Full points with combo maintained

**Flying Cube System**:
- Flying cubes move horizontally across rows in upper zone (y=19-23)
- Spawn from off-screen (x=-1 moving right, or x=13 moving left)
- Random color assignment on spawn: Yellow, Blue, Green, or White (25% each)
- Movement speed: 1.5-2.5 cells/sec (increases with waves)
- Spawn interval: 1.8 seconds base (decreases with waves)
- Flying cube appearance: Same gradient material as vertical cubes, with motion trail effect

**Flying Cube Matching Logic**:
- Flying cube participates in horizontal matches as it moves through its row in automatic zone
- Match condition: Flying cube color matches 2 or more same-colored cubes already in its row
- Match triggers when flying cube aligns with matching cubes (same y-coordinate)
- Matched cubes (including flying cube) display flashing outline, then explode
- Flying cube continues moving if no match occurs
- Multiple flying cubes can exist simultaneously in different rows

**Collision Logic**:
- Flying cube + Vertical cube: Flying cube stops if vertical cube blocks its path
- Stopped flying cube becomes part of the grid and can be tapped/moved if in preparation zone
- Flying cube + Flying cube: Both continue moving (pass through each other)
- Vertical cube moving upward + Flying cube: Vertical cube stops at collision point

**Wave Progression**:

- Wave 1-3: Flying cubes at 1.5 cells/sec, 2.0s spawn interval
- Wave 4-6: Flying cubes at 1.8 cells/sec, 1.7s spawn interval
- Wave 7-9: Flying cubes at 2.0 cells/sec, 1.5s spawn interval
- Wave 10+: +0.1 speed per wave, -0.1s spawn interval per wave

**Random Events** (every 30 seconds):
- Color Surge: All cubes change to random single color for 5 seconds
- Rainbow Rain: Spawn 5 cubes of each color simultaneously
- Flying Wave: Horizontal wave of 5 flying cubes spawns simultaneously
- Slowdown: Flying cube speed -50% for 5 seconds
- 2-second warning icon before event triggers

**Blizzard Event** (every 10 waves):

- Visual: Blue screen tint, particle effects

## 4. Business Rules and Logic

### 4.1 Zone System Rules
- Grid divided into two zones:
  - Preparation Zone: y=1 to y=8 (8 rows)
  - Automatic Zone: y=9 to y=24 (16 rows)
- Zone separator line displayed at y=8/y=9 boundary (bright yellow, semi-transparent, 2-pixel thickness)
- Preparation Zone rules:
  - Player can tap cubes to change colors
  - Player can swipe cubes horizontally to move them left/right
  - Player can swipe cubes vertically to move them up/down
  - Player can heat cubes to red state
  - Cubes continue moving upward automatically
  - Matches do NOT trigger (no cube removal)
  - This is a strategic preparation area for figure assembly
- Automatic Zone rules:
  - All player interactions disabled (taps and swipes ignored)
  - Cubes move upward automatically
  - Matches trigger when 3+ same-colored cubes align
  - Matched cubes explode and are removed
- Zone transition:
  - When a complete figure fully crosses from y=8 to y=9, it accelerates to 2× speed
  - Brief flash effect displayed when figure crosses separator
  - Tap counter resets if cube was being heated
  - Cube retains its current color after crossing

### 4.2 Figure Definition and Completion Logic
- Figure: 3 or more same-colored cubes connected by edges (horizontal or vertical adjacency)
- Edge connection: Two cubes share a common side (x±1 or y±1, not diagonal)
- Diagonal adjacency does NOT count as part of same figure
- Complete figure criteria:
  - All cubes in figure are edge-connected (form continuous chain)
  - No additional same-colored cubes are edge-connected to the figure
  - Surrounding cells contain either empty space or different-colored cubes
  - Diagonal adjacency to different-colored cubes is allowed
- Figure detection algorithm:
  - Scan all cubes in preparation zone
  - Group same-colored cubes by edge connectivity
  - Check if group has no additional edge-connected same-colored cubes
  - Mark group as complete figure if criteria met

### 4.3 Figure Acceleration System
- Acceleration trigger: Complete figure fully crosses separator line (all cubes have y≥9)
- Last cube rule: Acceleration activates only when the last cube of the figure enters y=9
- Partial crossing: If figure spans y=8 and y=9, acceleration does NOT occur
- Acceleration effect: All cubes in figure move at 2× speed (600ms per cell instead of 1200ms)
- Speed multiplier applies until:
  - Figure participates in a match and explodes
  - Figure reaches y=24 and stops
- Visual feedback: Speed lines, motion blur, or trailing particles on accelerated figures
- Strategic motivation: Smaller figures (3-4 cubes) cross faster and accelerate sooner than large figures
- Figure integrity: Accelerated figures maintain their structure during upward movement

### 4.4 Color System
- Four base colors: Yellow, Blue, Green, White
- Color cycle order: Yellow → Blue → Green → White → Yellow (loops)
- Single tap advances color by one step in cycle
- Color change takes effect immediately with smooth gradient transition
- No cooldown between color changes
- Red color (heated state) obtained only through 8 consecutive taps
- Color changes only possible in preparation zone (y=1-8)

### 4.5 Horizontal Movement System
- Swipe gesture on cube triggers horizontal movement
- Swipe left: Move cube to x-1 position
- Swipe right: Move cube to x+1 position
- Movement is instant with smooth slide animation (200ms duration)
- Movement blocked if:
  - Target cell occupied by another cube
  - Target position outside grid boundaries (x<1 or x>12)
  - Cube is in automatic zone (y≥9)
- Cube continues upward movement after horizontal repositioning
- No limit on number of horizontal movements per cube in preparation zone
- Visual feedback: Slide animation with motion trail effect
- Collision detection: Check target cell before movement execution

### 4.6 Vertical Movement System
- Swipe gesture on cube triggers vertical movement
- Swipe up: Move cube to y+1 position
- Swipe down: Move cube to y-1 position
- Movement is instant with smooth slide animation (200ms duration)
- Movement blocked if:
  - Target cell occupied by another cube
  - Target position outside preparation zone (y<1 or y>8)
  - Cube is in automatic zone (y≥9)
- Cube continues automatic upward movement after vertical repositioning
- No limit on number of vertical movements per cube in preparation zone
- Visual feedback: Slide animation with motion trail effect
- Collision detection: Check target cell before movement execution

### 4.7 Heating System
- Tap counter starts at 0 for each cube
- Each tap on same cube increments counter by 1
- Counter displays above cube as fraction (e.g., 3/8)
- At 8 taps within 2-second window: Cube turns red
- Red state is permanent until cube participates in match
- Tap counter resets to 0 if:
  - 2 seconds elapse between taps
  - Player taps different cube
  - Cube completes a match
  - Cube crosses into automatic zone (y≥9)
- Visual feedback:
  - Tap 1-3: Faint glow
  - Tap 4-6: Moderate glow with heat waves
  - Tap 7: Intense glow with sparks
  - Tap 8: Transform to red with particle burst and heat distortion
- Heating only possible in preparation zone (y=1-8)

### 4.8 Match Detection and Resolution (Automatic Zone Only)
- Match check performed every frame for all cubes in automatic zone (y≥9)
- Matches do NOT trigger in preparation zone (y=1-8)
- Horizontal match: Scan each row for 3+ consecutive same-color cubes
- Vertical match: Scan each column for 3+ consecutive same-color cubes
- Shape match: Detect Tetris-like patterns:
  - L-shape: 4 cubes forming L pattern
  - T-shape: 4 cubes forming T pattern
  - Z-shape: 4 cubes forming Z pattern
  - Square: 4 cubes forming 2×2 square
- Flying cube matching: Check if flying cube color matches 2+ same-colored cubes in its current row
- Red cube wildcard rule:
  - Red cube adopts color of adjacent cubes in potential match
  - If 2 Green + 1 Red in line: Counts as 3 Green
  - If 1 Red between 2 different colors: No match
  - Multiple red cubes in same match: Each acts as wildcard
- Match priority: Larger matches and shape matches processed first
- Simultaneous matches: All detected matches resolve in same frame
- Match resolution sequence:
  1. Identify all valid matches in automatic zone (horizontal, vertical, shapes)
  2. Display flashing outline effect on matched cubes for 0.5 seconds
  3. Calculate total points (base + shape bonus + combo + red bonus)
  4. Remove matched cubes with explosion particle effects
  5. Apply gravity (cubes above fall down)
  6. Check for cascade matches
  7. Increment combo counter
  8. Update score display
  9. Trigger reduced screen shake (33% intensity)
- Combo timer: 3-second window to create next match
- Combo multiplier progression:
  - 1st match: ×1.0 (base)
  - 2nd match: ×1.5
  - 3rd match: ×2.0
  - 4th+ match: ×2.5
- Combo resets if 3 seconds pass without new match
- Cascade matches maintain combo and award full points

### 4.9 Cube Spawning and Movement
- Spawn location: Random x-position from 1 to 12 at y=0
- Spawn color: Random selection from Yellow, Blue, Green, White (25% each)
- Spawn interval progression:
  - Wave 1-3: 500ms between spawns
  - Wave 4-6: 400ms between spawns
  - Wave 7-9: 300ms between spawns
  - Wave 10+: 200ms between spawns
- Movement: Each cube moves upward in its spawned vertical lane
- Base movement interval: 1200ms per cell
- Accelerated movement interval: 600ms per cell (for figures that fully crossed separator)
- Movement stops when:
  - Cube reaches y=24 (top boundary)
  - Next cell above is occupied by another cube
  - Cube collides with flying cube
- Stopped cubes remain in place and form stacking structure
- Cubes can be tapped and moved horizontally/vertically while in preparation zone (y=1-8)

### 4.10 Gravity and Falling Mechanics
- When match clears cubes in automatic zone, all cubes above the cleared position fall down
- Falling speed: 600ms per cell (2× normal movement speed)
- Cubes fall until:
  - They reach another stopped cube below
  - They reach y=0 (bottom)
  - They complete falling distance
- Falling cubes can form new matches during fall
- Multiple columns can fall simultaneously
- Falling animation is smooth and continuous
- Falling cubes maintain their locked/unlocked state based on current y-position

### 4.11 Flying Cube Spawning and Movement
- Spawn location: x=-1 (moving right) or x=13 (moving left)
- Spawn y-range: 19-23
- Base spawn interval: 1.8 seconds
- Spawn interval reduction: -0.1s per wave after wave 3
- Minimum spawn interval: 0.5 seconds
- Color distribution on spawn: Yellow, Blue, Green, White (25% each)
- Movement speed formula: base_speed + (wave × speed_per_wave)
  - Base: 1.5 cells/sec
  - Increment: 0.1 cells/sec per wave
- Flying cube removed when:
  - Participates in match and explodes
  - Moves off-screen (x<-1 or x>13)
  - Collides with vertical cube and stops

### 4.12 Collision Resolution
- Check performed every frame for cubes in y=19-23 range
- Collision detected when flying cube and vertical cube occupy same cell
- Resolution:
  - Flying cube stops at collision point
  - Stopped flying cube becomes part of the grid
  - Can be tapped to change color or moved horizontally/vertically if in preparation zone
  - Can be heated if in preparation zone
  - Blocks horizontal movement for other flying cubes
- Vertical cube moving upward into flying cube path: Vertical cube stops

### 4.13 Scoring Calculation
- Base match points:
  - 3 cubes: 30 points
  - 4 cubes: 60 points
  - 5 cubes: 100 points
  - 6 cubes: 150 points
  - 7 cubes: 200 points
  - 8+ cubes: 200 + (50 × (count - 7)) points
- Shape match bonus:
  - L-shape: +50 points
  - T-shape: +50 points
  - Z-shape: +50 points
  - Square: +100 points
- Combo multiplier applied to base points
- Red cube bonus: +20 points per red cube in match
- Cascade match: Full points with combo maintained
- Total score formula: ((base_points + shape_bonus) × combo_multiplier) + red_bonus

### 4.14 Wave Progression Logic
- Wave increments every 60 seconds of active gameplay
- Difficulty scaling:
  - Flying cube speed: +0.1 cells/sec per wave
  - Spawn interval: -0.1s per wave (minimum 0.5s)
  - Cube spawn rate increases with wave progression
- Blizzard trigger: Every 10th wave (10, 20, 30, etc.)

### 4.15 Random Event System
- Event trigger: Every 30 seconds of active gameplay
- Event selection: Random from available pool
- Warning phase: 2-second icon flash before activation
- Event types and effects:
  - Color Surge: All active cubes change to same random color for 5 seconds
  - Rainbow Rain: Spawn 5 cubes of each color (20 total) over 3 seconds
  - Flying Wave: Spawn 5 flying cubes simultaneously at random y in 19-23 range
  - Slowdown: Multiply flying cube speed by 0.5 for 5 seconds
- Events do not stack (new event cancels previous if overlapping)

### 4.16 Blizzard Event
- Trigger condition: Wave number divisible by 10
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Effects:
  - Flying cube spawn interval ×0.5 (twice as frequent)
  - Cube spawn rate +50%
  - Visual: Blue screen overlay, increased particle density
- Warning: 3-second countdown before activation

### 4.17 Game Over Conditions
- Primary: Any vertical lane fills completely from y=0 to y=24 with stopped cubes
- Secondary: Player manually quits to menu
- On Game Over:
  - Stop all cube spawning and movement
  - Trigger death sequence (slowdown, effects)
  - Calculate final statistics
  - Check for new records
  - Display Game Over Screen

### 4.18 Continue Mechanic
- Available once per session
- Cost: Watch rewarded ad OR spend 50 crystals
- Effect: Clear all cubes in top 3 rows (y=22-24), resume at current wave with current score

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

### 6.1 Core Gameplay
- Cubes spawn randomly at y=0 across x-positions 1-12 with equal color distribution
- Each cube moves upward in its spawned vertical lane at 1200ms per cell (base speed)
- Cubes stop when reaching y=24 or colliding with cube above
- Stopped cubes remain in place and accumulate vertically
- Accelerated figures move at 600ms per cell (2× speed)

### 6.2 Zone System
- Grid displays horizontal separator line at y=8/y=9 boundary (bright yellow, 2-pixel thickness, opacity 0.6)
- Preparation zone (y=1-8) allows all player interactions (tap, horizontal swipe, vertical swipe)
- Automatic zone (y=9-24) blocks all player interactions
- Matches do NOT trigger in preparation zone
- Matches trigger normally in automatic zone
- Cubes crossing from y=8 to y=9 display brief flash effect
- Tap counter resets when cube crosses to automatic zone
- Taps and swipes on cubes in automatic zone are ignored

### 6.3 Color Cycling
- Single tap on cube in preparation zone cycles color through Yellow → Blue → Green → White → Yellow
- Color change applies instantly with smooth gradient transition
- Taps on cubes in automatic zone produce no effect
- No cooldown between color changes
- Visual feedback: Brief glow pulse on color change

### 6.4 Horizontal Movement
- Swipe left on cube in preparation zone moves cube to x-1 position
- Swipe right on cube in preparation zone moves cube to x+1 position
- Movement executes with smooth slide animation (200ms duration)
- Movement blocked if target cell occupied or outside boundaries
- Swipes on cubes in automatic zone produce no effect
- Cube continues upward movement after horizontal repositioning
- Visual feedback: Slide animation with motion trail

### 6.5 Vertical Movement
- Swipe up on cube in preparation zone moves cube to y+1 position
- Swipe down on cube in preparation zone moves cube to y-1 position
- Movement executes with smooth slide animation (200ms duration)
- Movement blocked if target cell occupied or outside preparation zone (y<1 or y>8)
- Swipes on cubes in automatic zone produce no effect
- Cube continues automatic upward movement after vertical repositioning
- Visual feedback: Slide animation with motion trail

### 6.6 Figure Definition and Completion
- Figure consists of 3+ same-colored cubes connected by edges (not diagonals)
- Edge connection: Cubes share common side (horizontally or vertically adjacent)
- Diagonal adjacency does NOT count as part of same figure
- Complete figure: All cubes edge-connected, no additional same-colored cubes edge-connected
- System correctly identifies complete figures in preparation zone

### 6.7 Figure Acceleration System
- Complete figure accelerates to 2× speed when fully crossing separator line
- Acceleration activates only when last cube of figure enters y=9
- Partial crossing (figure spans y=8 and y=9) does NOT trigger acceleration
- Accelerated figures move at 600ms per cell
- Visual feedback: Speed lines or motion blur on accelerated figures
- Acceleration persists until figure participates in match or reaches y=24
- Smaller figures (3-4 cubes) cross faster than larger figures

### 6.8 Heating System
- 8 consecutive taps within 2 seconds turn cube red (only in preparation zone)
- Tap counter displays above cube as fraction
- Red cube glows with red-orange particles and heat distortion
- Tap counter resets when cube crosses to automatic zone
- Red state persists until cube participates in match
- Heating only possible in preparation zone

### 6.9 Visual Design
- Grid displays thin semi-transparent white lines (opacity 0.15) on dark gradient background
- Zone separator line clearly visible (bright yellow, 2-pixel thickness, opacity 0.6)
- All cubes use gradient materials with smooth color transitions
- Cube colors match specified gradient palettes
- Cubes have glossy finish and rounded edges
- Background gradient transitions from deep blue to black

### 6.10 Match System (Automatic Zone Only)
- 3+ aligned cubes of same color (horizontal or vertical) trigger match in automatic zone
- Tetris-like shape patterns (L, T, Z, Square) trigger shape matches in automatic zone
- Matched cubes display flashing outline effect for 0.5 seconds before explosion
- Outline color matches cube color with increased brightness
- Flash frequency: 3 pulses per 0.5 seconds
- After outline animation, cubes explode with particle burst
- Red cubes act as wildcards and complete matches with any color
- Match detection occurs every frame for all cubes in automatic zone only
- No matches trigger in preparation zone
- Multiple simultaneous matches resolve correctly
- Combo counter increments on consecutive matches
- Combo multiplier applies correctly (×1.0, ×1.5, ×2.0, ×2.5)
- Combo resets after 3 seconds without new match

### 6.11 Match Visual Effects
- 3-cube match: Single outline flash, small particle burst
- 4-cube match: Double outline flash, medium particle burst with sparkles
- 5+ cube match: Triple outline flash, large particle burst with explosion wave
- Shape match: Special outline pattern, enhanced particle effects
- Screen shake reduced to 33% of original intensity
- Screen shake intensity scales with match size

### 6.12 Gravity and Cascade
- When match clears cubes in automatic zone, cubes above fall down to fill gap
- Falling cubes move at 600ms per cell
- Falling cubes stop when reaching another cube or y=0
- Cascade matches form during falling and award full points
- Cascade matches maintain combo multiplier
- Multiple columns can fall simultaneously
- Falling animation is smooth and continuous

### 6.13 Scoring

- 4-cube match awards 60 points
- 5-cube match awards 100 points
- 6-cube match awards 150 points
- 7+ cube matches award 200 + 50 per additional cube
- Shape match bonus: +50 for L/T/Z, +100 for Square
- Red cube in match adds +20 bonus points
- Combo multiplier applies to base points correctly
- Cascade matches award full points with combo maintained

### 6.14 Flying Cube System
- Flying cubes spawn at x=-1 or x=13 in y-range 19-23
- Flying cubes assigned random color on spawn (25% each)
- Flying cubes move horizontally at speeds matching wave difficulty
- Flying cubes display motion trail effect
